# Third-party software

Household Ledger includes one third-party library, unmodified, in the `vendor/` folder:

- **PDF.js** (`vendor/pdf.min.js`, `vendor/pdf.worker.min.js`), version 3.11.174, by Mozilla and contributors.
  Licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**: <https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>.
  Source and licence: <https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js>.

It is used on your device to read the text out of PDF files. The PolyForm Noncommercial licence in `LICENSE` covers Household Ledger's own code only, not PDF.js.
